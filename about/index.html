About

Melanie Cebula is a classically trained musician, multi-instrumentalist, and producer based in Los Angeles. Previously, she spent several years working in cloud infrastructure, where she focused on making large-scale systems more efficient and usable. In 2025 she released her first EP, "gospel for survival," under the name Sea of Diane, and in 2026 she released her second EP, "all about u," under her producer name melliejellie. She also performs live handpan soundbaths, serves on a nonprofit board, and is active in her local climbing, foraging, and yoga communities.
